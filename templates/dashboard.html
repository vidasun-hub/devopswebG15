{{ icon('book') }} Behind the numbers+

For N values, μ is their mean.

Mean
μ = Σx / N
Population variance
σ² = Σ(x − μ)² / N
Population standard deviation
σ = √σ²

Median is the middle sorted value, or the average of the two middle values. All equally frequent repeated modes are shown. Distinct values have no mode; a single value is its own mode.
